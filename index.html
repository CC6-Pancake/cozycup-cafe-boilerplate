Our Story

A cafe that started with one good pot of coffee.

Cozy Cup Cafe began as a two-table counter and a single drip pot, run on the idea that a neighborhood deserves a place worth slowing down for. Every cup is still made the same way it was on day one — with care, and no shortcuts.

Today we roast in small batches, bake before the sun's up, and keep the door open for anyone who needs a warm seat and a good cup. Same corner, same care, just more chairs.

"We're not trying to be the fastest cup in town — just the one you look forward to."
